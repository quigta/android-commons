Privacy Policy for Sampoorna Sanskriti

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

Sampoorna Sanskriti ("the App") is built as a Free and Ad-Free application. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is. This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

The App is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any of your personal data on external servers. All your data remains strictly on your local device.

However, the App requires certain device permissions to provide its core features:

  • Location Data (Approximate & Precise): The App requests location access solely to calculate highly accurate local astronomical timings (Panchang, Sunrise, Sunset, Moonrise, Moonset). This calculation happens entirely offline on your device. Your location is never sent to us or any third party.
  • Camera & Storage (Photos): The App allows you to attach photos to your custom rituals and checklists. These photos are saved locally on your device's storage. We do not access, upload, or view your photos.
  • Notifications: The App schedules local alarms to remind you of upcoming festivals and rituals. These are triggered locally by your phone's operating system.

2. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Because we do not collect any user data, we do not share any data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics companies. There are no third-party tracking SDKs (like Google Analytics or Facebook Pixel) installed in this App.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

All data you create (custom rituals, checklists, preferences) is stored in the App's local database. You can delete this data at any time by:

  • Deleting a specific ritual from within the App.
  • Clearing the App's data from your device's Settings.
  • Uninstalling the App.

4. Children's Privacy

Our Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13, as we do not collect personal information from anyone.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us by opening an issue on our GitHub repository: github.com/quigta/sampoorna-sanskriti.
